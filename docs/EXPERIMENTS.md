# Experiment history and evidence

CoNN asks whether known or learned associative relationships can improve computation and learning. Existing studies test fixed geometry and externally conditioned gating. Findings below are **historical recorded results**, not fresh statistical recalculation or independent reproduction during cleanup.

Accuracy and macro-F1 differences are **percentage points (pp)**; positive favors the first condition. Primary/secondary analyses, exploratory development and final tests remain distinct.

## Findings that qualify the headlines

**Fixed versus scheduled locality.** In 1B, D−S was −2.65625 pp, CI [−4.4293,−0.8832]; fixed-local S outranked scheduled D. In 1C, S’s superiority was not retained, and a fixed medium-width condition had the largest descriptive AND mean. In Phase 2 development, fixed d50=.25 exceeded scheduled Gaussian by +0.92913 pp in the saved seed-level audit. These separate comparisons do not identify a universally best policy. See [1C](../conn_experiment_01/results/runs/20261018T024146Z_experiment1c_v1/research_report.md) and [saved audit statistics](../AUDIT_20261020/Supporting_Evidence/recalculated_statistics.json).

**Alignment and transfer.** 1C’s secondary AND D−shuffle was +2.53906 pp, CI [1.1976,3.8805]; XOR reversed direction: −2.53906 pp, CI [−4.5727,−0.5054]. Engineered HAR’s primary was negative. Favorable synthetic findings do not establish cross-task superiority.

**Development context.** Phase 2/3’s selected-LR subject-bootstrap intervals are descriptive: Gaussian−dense [0.0743,1.1963] pp; Gaussian−shuffle [0.4350,1.9800] pp. Selection reuse and the original scaler’s reuse across development folds limit confirmation. Phase 3’s factor stayed near one; its context increment did not resolve benefit. Neither completion nor factor allocation demonstrates learned memory.

**Phase 3B numerical findings.** Its report records Level 1 in 35/35 C4 cases and Level 2 in 49/175, rather than universal functional sensitivity. The five seed primary differences have three positive and two negative signs; their t interval includes zero, unlike the published subject-bootstrap interval. Reported 140/140 exact same-seed final states/predictions are historical local replay checks. The [post-outcome reconciliation](../CoNN_Associative_Relationships/phase3b_context_diagnostics/reports/PROTOCOL_RECONCILIATION_20261020.md) cannot retrospectively preregister the run.

**Experiment 04 controls and endpoints.** Aligned−uniform is +0.84473 pp but Holm p=.117188, so magnitude is not decisively excluded. The negative-task contrast is −0.02441 pp (Holm p=.957031); failure to reject is not equivalence. The structured-minus-negative interaction is +1.15723 pp (Holm p=.048828), a secondary result. Immediate fixed-weight release remains unresolved: +0.53711 pp, CI [−0.1782,1.2524], Holm p=.25. Positive final performance after 480 dense updates does not establish immediate persistence. See [statistical definitions](research_validation/experiment_04/STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS.md) and [limitations](research_validation/experiment_04/LIMITATIONS.md).

## Evidence and interpretation

“Independent replication” in some reports means new seeds within this project. Experiment 04’s “independent generalization” means held-out examples within its DGP. Neither establishes separately conducted external reproduction. Saved passing tests and matching hashes establish narrower facts than a valid scientific hypothesis.

The [dated scientific audit](../AUDIT_20261020/FINAL_AUDIT_SUMMARY.md) and [recalculation source](../AUDIT_20261020/Supporting_Evidence/independent_recalculation.py) remain original evidence. A bootstrap attribution gap and separate review checksum discrepancy are described in [reproducibility](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).
