## 2. Precisely define the causal hypotheses

### H1 — Symmetry restriction

Identical rank-component columns constrain the attenuation matrix to rank at most one.

Prediction: symmetry-breaking initialization should increase effective attenuation rank, but it need not improve learning or prediction.

### H2 — Weak-gradient dynamics

Sigmoid saturation and bilinear factor coupling may produce small gradients.

Prediction: less-saturated initialization increases gradient and update magnitudes, although predictive usefulness may remain unchanged.

### H3 — Numerical representability

Some parameter updates disappear in float32.

Prediction: float64 master parameters preserve updates, but improvements to forward behavior occur only if the resulting C changes survive conversion to model dtype.

### H4 — Architectural redundancy

The shared weight matrices and attenuation-only contextual gate may allow the base network to compensate for modulation.

Prediction: context parameters can change substantially without providing additional predictive value.

### H5 — Limited contextual information

Sensor availability may be recoverable from the masked inputs.

Prediction: correctly supplied context codes offer little benefit over masked non-context models, even with numerically active gates.

Do not declare H4 or H5 proven from failure to improve classification. Distinguish evidence of redundancy from unresolved optimization limitations.

## 4. Quantitative numerical criteria

Freeze tolerances before training.

### Level 1 — Numerical learning

Require all of the following:

1. Finite, analytically consistent gradients.
2. At least one actual context-parameter update beyond storage rounding.
3. A measurable modulation change on live-support entries.
4. No violation of the declared C bounds.

Use `max_abs_delta_C >= 1e−6` on live-support entries as the provisional float32 forward-level threshold for a positive learning claim.

Additionally report float64 changes below this threshold without calling them successful float32 forward learning.

### C3 futility criterion

After the declared pilot, if C3 has representable float64 parameter updates but `max_abs_delta_C < 1e−6` in the actual forward dtype on live-support entries, classify C3 as failing the functional numerical criterion.

Stop additional C3 full-grid fits under the preregistered futility rule.

Retain its pilot measurements, log the omitted fits, and do not substitute another model.

### Level 2 — Functional context differentiation

Require:

- Different learned attenuation matrices on their comparable live-support entries.
- Pairwise normalized Frobenius differences exceeding a frozen engineering floor of 1e−4.
- An effect exceeding the 95th percentile of the corresponding shuffled-code diagnostic null.
- Measurable same-weight output changes beyond established numerical inference tolerance.

Report the distribution of all three pairwise context contrasts.

For comparisons between contexts, use the intersection of their live-support entries. Analyze non-intersecting entries separately.

Do not claim Level 2 if the only detected variation comes from first-layer masking artifacts.

### Level 3 — Predictive benefit

Primary contrast:

Gaussian C4 minus Gaussian masked, no learned context.

Require:

- Mean paired macro-F1 improvement of at least +0.005.
- Lower endpoint of the descriptive 95% subject-bootstrap interval greater than zero.
- Valid Level 2 evidence if the conclusion specifically attributes the benefit to learned context differentiation.

Label the result an exploratory development success, never independent confirmation.

Numerical thresholds are operational research choices, not established universal standards for neural representation learning.
